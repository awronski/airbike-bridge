How to use · one Power sensor

One-time Garmin pairing

  1. On your fēnix 6, hold MENU → Sensors & Accessories → Add New → Power.
  2. Select AirBike Bridge. This one sensor supplies power, speed, cadence and distance. A separate Speed/Cadence entry is not needed.

Every ride

  1. Power the ATOM near the bike and press Connect on the bike.
  2. Start Bike Indoor on the Garmin and pedal.

The relay runs without a phone, Mac or open web page. Wi-Fi is used for this dashboard. A virtual 2000 mm wheel converts bike speed into Garmin distance; your watch's speed has been checked against the console.

Start, Stop and Resume control dashboard recording only. Stop freezes ride totals; live readings and Garmin forwarding continue. Manual Stop requires Resume. Automatic controls default to 3 seconds of pedalling to start/resume and 10 seconds without pedalling to pause. Speed from a coasting fan does not count as pedalling. Save & finish is in Options; rides also save on bike disconnection, a console reset or 10 minutes without pedalling. Clear ride discards only the current dashboard session. An unfinished current session is kept in RAM; saved history is kept in flash.

iPhone · open without Safari bars

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. If shown, enable Open as Web App. Launch AirBike from that new icon to use the dashboard without Safari's address and tab bars.

Stay on the same local Wi-Fi as the ATOM. An ordinary Safari tab keeps its browser controls.

Change Wi-Fi

  1. Hold the ATOM's front button for four seconds.
  2. On your phone, join the network named AirBike-Setup-xxxxxx.
  3. Open http://192.168.77.1/ and enter your new 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi name and password.
  4. After it connects, return to your normal Wi-Fi and open airbike-atom.local or the new address shown by setup.

The password is entered only on the local setup page. Keep your Mac on its normal network if you want to stay connected to Codex during setup.
